Ethical and Accountable Governance
Square maintains a governance framework built on ethics, transparency, and strict regulatory compliance.
The company aligns its practices with the Corporate Governance Code 2018 issued by the Bangladesh
Securities and Exchange Commission, ensuring all requirements are met. This commitment is shown
through key disclosures, including the statement under Clause 1(5), profiles of directors eligible for
reappointment, the Management Discussion and Analysis, and certifications from both the CEO and CFO.
These elements show how seriously the company upholds strong governance principles.
These disclosures appear across Annexure I to VI in the Annual Report, offering a clear and structured view
of how the company maintains accountability. The Compliance Auditors’ certification further strengthens
trust by confirming that all conditions have been met. Together, these documents highlight Square
dedication to a transparent and responsible system that supports stability, protects stakeholder interests,
and reinforces its reputation as a well-governed organization.
Governance Structure
Square Pharma’s governance system is structured across policy and strategy, operations, performance
assessment, and stewardship. This framework supports ongoing decision-making by combining top-down
delegation with bottom-up reporting so managers and employees can act responsibly while leadership
maintains clear oversight.
Governance responsibilities are shared by the Board, Executive Management, and operational teams. Their
combined efforts help the company pursue sustainable growth and protect stakeholder interests. All
governance practices follow ethical and moral standards that reflect the socio-political and environmental
context of Bangladesh.

Measurement against Counterfeit Medicines
Blister Changes
Counterfeit medicines often show abnormal blister features, including uneven sealing, unusual colors,
poor-quality printing, or mismatched batch numbers. Subtle texture differences also help identify fake
products quickly.
Authentication Scans
Authentication scans use barcodes, QR codes, holograms, or digital verification apps to confirm
product legitimacy. These tools help consumers, pharmacists, and regulators detect counterfeit
medicines with greater accuracy.

Practices in the Cephalosporin Plant
The plant operates under a high-control environment with careful monitoring of air quality, equipment
hygiene, waste handling, and material movement. Each stage of production is validated to keep the
antibiotics potent, safe, and compliant with global manufacturing standards. These practices help limit
unnecessary exposure of antimicrobial substances to the environment. The Cephalosporin unit is
physically separated from all other manufacturing zones. Dedicated air-handling systems, isolated
production rooms, independent staff access, separate utilities, and closed-loop processes reduce any
chance of cross-mixing with non-antibiotic products. This separation supports responsible antibiotic
manufacturing and strengthens our stewardship efforts.
